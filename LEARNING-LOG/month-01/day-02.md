Trigger: a patient pays for a hospital card at OPD.
Step 1: the cashier writes the patients name, age and gender on a record book and the clinic card.
Step 2: the cashier then refer the patient to record for file opening.
Step 3: the record department then rewrite the patients name on a flat folder.
Step 4: the record clerk takes the folder to the doctor in the consulting room.
Step 5: the patient waits outside the consultation room for their name to be called.
End result: the patient gets to be consulted by a doctor.
Pain points: Step 1 is analog thus making backup difficult and likewise time consuming, 
step 2 is inefficient in case of emmergency and also prone to missing of folder or damage due to poor handling,
step 3 involve rewriting and making a room for possible typo errors and slow proceeding,
Step 4 opens up the posibility of sending the folder to a wrong consulting room therefore making experience worst,
At the end of the day, the process makes the patients experience less satisfactory and also the record handling process hecktic.
